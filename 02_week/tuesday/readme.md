## Overview
People who don't want to scroll should be able to tell what your project is about at a glance.

- Problem statement/info
- Background info
- Justification (why does this project matter?)
- Overview of your results

## Executive summary

#### Purpose
- Reiterate your problem statement
- "I set out to find X, Y, and Z"
- Can also reiterate some background/justification.

#### Methods
- What did you DO? How did you change the data? 
- How did this contribute to your goal?
- Explain yourself any time you make a big decision: "Eliminated first half of the alphabet, because the letters I want are at the end."
- Later on, you may split this section into subsections: "EDA", "Modeling", etc.
